01
Trace Foot Outline

Place a blank paper on a flat floor against a solid wall wall. Step on it firmly and trace the exact perimeter shape of your bare foot with a vertical pencil tracking line.

02
Measure Heel-to-Toe Length

Use a standard metric ruler to gauge the straight distance from the absolute furthest edge of your heel curve to the tip of your longest toe in precise millimeters.

03
Factor Brand Width Slack

Compare your millimeter length to the sizing conversion grids below. Add exactly 5mm of extra safety spacing buffer room if you prefer a slightly relaxed comfort padding lock.

// Preservation Protocols

Material Sourcing Care Index

Direct laboratory cleaning benchmarks to maintain footwear fiber longevity across different texture surfaces.

SILHOUETTE-REF: #LTR-MNT

Full-Grain & Calfskin Leather

Requires regular hydration to prevent deep crease cracking. Never submerge full-grain leather surfaces under active water loops.

Cleaning Tool: Horsehair Brush / Microfiber
Conditioner Run: Every 4 - 6 Weeks (Wax Base)
Drying Protocol: Natural Room Temp (Air Dry)

Napped Suede & Nubuck

Highly sensitive to moisture fields. Active liquid contact crushes the natural fibrous nap textures instantly.

Cleaning Tool: Suede Eraser / Brass Crepe Brush
Protection Guard: Hydrophobic Spray Shield
Stain Rescue: Dry Rubbing Only (No Liquid)

// Athletic Mesh & Knit Extraction Workflow

01

Dry Surface Brushing

Utilize a stiff-bristle brush to dislodge dry surface mud particles from the mesh matrices before introducing any liquid compounds.

02

Solution Application

Mix lukewarm water with mild specialized sneaker solution. Dip a soft-bristle brush and agitate the mesh fabric fields gently in circular motion bubbles.

03

Microfiber Blotting

Do not rinse under pressurized taps. Press a clean dry microfiber towel onto the wet fabric layers repeatedly to absorb contaminated dirty water.

04

Internal Stuffing Rest

Stuff shoe interiors with plain crumpled paper towels to retain original structural toe-box profile contours during the 24-hour drying cycle line.
