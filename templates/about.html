// Our Testing Methodology Protocols

01 / Zero Sponsorships

Unbiased Acquisition

Every single sneaker or formal shoe reviewed on Vowelrate is purchased directly at retail market value. We strictly reject corporate brand sponsorships to guarantee 100% honest, uninfluenced rating outputs.

02 / Dynamic Mileage

The 50-Mile Wear Rule

We do not perform superficial desktop reviews. Our team puts a minimum of 50 active testing miles into sports silhouettes and 3 weeks of intensive wear inside formal models before generating safety scores.

03 / Lab Verification

Biomechanical Metrics

Using specialized instrumentation, we gauge real shoe weight metrics, sole deflection stiffness angles, and seam durability thresholds under heavy pressure load loops to give users precise structural insights.
