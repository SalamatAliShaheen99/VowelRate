// Rapid Diagnostic Troubleshooting Sheets

01 / FIT ISSUE

Shoe Feels Too Tight?

If your newly unboxed sneaker causes midfoot arch strain under active testing, remove the factory stock insole layer completely or substitute it with a thinner 2mm low-profile orthotic pad to open vertical stack space.

02 / LEATHER CREASE

Preventing Early Cracking?

Full-grain leather fibers dry out rapidly under harsh outdoor atmospheric conditions. Apply a thin coat of wax-based structural leather conditioner every 4 weeks to stabilize interior collagen fiber matrices handles.

03 / FABRIC ODOR

Eliminating Mesh Odors?

Sweat salts trap organic bacteria fields inside shoe linings. Never utilize intense high-heat washing machines; instead, inject dry baking soda powder matrices inside the canvas to absorb moisture compounds safely.
